By leveraging multimodal inputs, developers may be able to augment textual data with visual context, potentially improving performance in scenarios where pure text data is sparse or insufficient. The "Small" designation suggests that the model is optimized for lower computational overhead, which is a critical requirement for researchers and practitioners working with limited hardware infrastructure.
